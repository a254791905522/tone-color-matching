About Tone Color Matching

Tone Color Matching is an offline color matching puzzle built with SpriteKit. Each level presents a target color, and your task is to recreate that color as accurately as possible using Hue, Saturation, and Lightness sliders.

Progress through 50 levels organized into five themed sets. Early levels show a preview of the target color and a memory hint. Later levels hide the preview, challenging you to recall the exact shade from memory.

Your score is based on DeltaE, the scientific color difference metric. The closer your color is to the target, the higher your score and the more stars you earn.

How to Play

1

Read the Brief

Each level shows the target name and a short hint describing the color.

2

Study the Target

Levels 1-5 show a preview swatch. Levels 6+ hide it, testing your color memory.

3

Adjust Hue

Drag the Hue slider (0-360 degrees) to find the right color family.

4

Set Saturation

Drag the Saturation slider (0-100%) to control color intensity.

5

Set Lightness

Drag the Lightness slider (0-100%) to make the color darker or lighter.

6

Submit & Score

Tap Submit. Score is based on DeltaE. Earn 1-3 stars and unlock the next level.
